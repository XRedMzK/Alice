# Working policy for the public Alice source tree

This is a clean source publication, not the former private research workspace.
Follow the current owner's task and the implemented-versus-planned boundary in
[ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md). Keep Foundation contracts and historical
evidence intact. Changes to frozen contracts require explicit versioned review.

Use science to design the architecture; use engineering evidence to determine
whether Alice works. Validation must be proportional to the concrete claim and
risk. Ordinary deterministic fixes normally need affected engineering tests,
not a new scientific qualification. Reuse unchanged evidence.

No memory, Memory Bridge, ComfyUI, M2, model download, training or architecture
experiment is implicitly authorized by the roadmap. Use the explicit no-training
basic command in README. Preserve upstream licenses and provenance; never publish
private material, model assets or credentials. Do not alter the archived M1 history.

The historical Foundation documents can refer to an older `CODEX.md`, `temp.md`
authority or original workspace evidence. This new public working policy does not
replace those historical records or retroactively change their accepted meaning.
