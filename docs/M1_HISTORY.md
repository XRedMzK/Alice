# M1 research separation

On 24 October 2026 the owner ended the mandatory active M1 research programme.
M1 is **RESEARCH ARCHIVED / NOT_FROZEN** and continues as an independent open
research project accepting external contributions:

- [M1-Event-Understanding](https://github.com/XRedMzK/M1-Event-Understanding).
- [Research snapshot, 24 October 2026](https://github.com/XRedMzK/M1-Event-Understanding/releases/tag/research-snapshot-20261024).

It investigated a bounded current-turn event representation, with LOC, TYPE,
BIND, POL and REL and supporting canonicalization. Scoped positive results and
failed/incomplete qualifications remain what they were. LOC/BIND and full M1
integration were not closed by archiving. A historically frozen POL component
retains only its original qualification scope. Event structures were not shown
fundamentally impossible, and TYPE7 was not proved a universal semantic interface.

Alice's active direction is now functional composition (Collab) with separately
owned neural memory and a frozen replaceable language model. That direction is
planned; it does not erase M1 provenance or constitute a new working runtime.
No M1 caches, source packages, checkpoints, private chats or experimental runs are
dependencies of this clean Foundation tree. Its published history is not modified.
